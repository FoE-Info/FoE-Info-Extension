# **Great Buildings Meta & Leveling Guide**

## **1\. Introduction to Great Buildings & Prestige Tiers**

**Great Buildings (GBs)** are unique, highly impactful structures in Forge of Empires that provide your city with passive and active bonuses—ranging from Forge Points (FPs) and goods to massive military attack/defense, guild goods, and specialized utility abilities. Unlike standard city structures, Great Buildings are upgraded beyond their base stats by depositing Forge Points and unlocking level tiers using sets of **Blueprints (BPs)**.

### Prestige Tier System

To address power creep from modern event structures, Great Building progression is divided into three distinct prestige tiers:

* **Copper Tier**: Covers Levels 1 to 200\.  
* **Silver Tier**: Unlocks at Level 101 and maxes out at Level 300\.  
* **Gold Tier**: Unlocks at Level 201 and hard-caps at **Level 400** (the absolute maximum cap for any GB).  
* **19 Selected Tiered GBs**: Only 19 of the most popular GBs—including Statue of Zeus, Cathedral of Aachen, Castel del Monte, The Arc, Arctic Orangery, Alcatraz, Blue Galaxy, Château Frontenac, Himeji Castle, The Kraken, Observatory, Terracotta Army, Seed Vault, Space Carrier, St. Mark's Basilica, Deal Castle, Lotus Temple, Colosseum, and The Virgo Project—receive all three tiers (Copper, Silver, and Gold).  
* **29 Copper-Only GBs**: All other 29 GBs (such as Cape Canaveral or Innovation Tower) remain capped at **Level 200** in the Copper Tier.  
* **Multiplicative Army Boosts**: Four Gold-tier GBs—**Statue of Zeus**, **Cathedral of Aachen**, **Alcatraz**, and **The Kraken**—grant multiplicative army boosts that multiply your total existing combat stats rather than adding flat bonuses.  
* **Blueprints & The Refinery**: Unlocking Level 101+ requires Silver/Gold Blueprints, coins, and current-era goods. Higher-tier BPs are acquired by investing in high-level GBs or using the **Blueprint Refinery** to trade lower-tier BPs (up to 5 daily refinements per GB).

## **2\. Core Meta Rankings**

### Tier 1: Essential Core ("Must-Haves")

* **The Arc**: The single highest priority GB to rush to Level 80\. Provides a **\+90% bonus** (1.9× multiplier) on all GB contribution rewards, unlocks 1.9 thread participation, fuels your FP inventory, and provides massive guild goods to your treasury.  
* **Blue Galaxy (BG)**: Gives a percentage chance to **double collections** (up to 14 doubled collections per day) on top special and event buildings. Highly effective when paired with Finish Special Production (FSP) items to rapidly yield high-value building fragments.  
* **Château Frontenac (CF)**: Multiplies non-FP quest rewards (coins, supplies, goods, medals, and diamonds). Powers recurring quest loops (such as the *Unbirthday Party* quest) and yields thousands of extra diamonds from story and side quests.  
* **Arctic Orangery (AO)**: Delivers a **Critical Hit chance** (150% damage) against enemy units of the same era alongside daily Forge Points.

### Tier 2: Core Military & Guild Utility

* **The Combat Trinity (Statue of Zeus, Cathedral of Aachen, Castel del Monte)**: Compact, essential sources of military attack and defense boosts.  
* **Alcatraz (Traz)**: Generates large daily pools of unattached military units based on the military barracks placed in your city (which do not require road connections).  
* **Guild Treasury Utility (Observatory & AI Core)**: Efficient, compact sources of guild goods to supply Guild Battlegrounds and Guild Expedition costs.  
* **Seed Vault & Temple of Relics**: Essential utility for diamond farmers via aiding rewards and Guild Expedition Relic drops.

### Tier 3: Obsolete & "Newbie Trap" GBs

Due to the power creep of modern event structures, older GBs that only produce coins, supplies, or flat happiness provide far lower stats per tile than event buildings. **Colosseum**, **Lotus Temple**, **Space Needle**, **Capitol**, **Gaea Statue**, **Oracle of Delphi**, **Habitat**, **Notre Dame**, **Hagia Sophia**, and **Frauenkirche of Dresden** are widely considered inefficient drains on city space and Forge Points.

## **3\. 1.9 Leveling & Priming Mechanics**

### Leveling Ecosystems & Strategies

* **Self-Leveling**: Depositing your own FPs directly into your GB. Highly wasteful because all 5 reward spots (and their associated FPs, medals, and blueprints) are completely lost.  
* **Swap Threads**: Guild message threads where players deposit set amounts into the building posted directly above theirs. Widely considered an inefficient "newbie trap" because they fail to utilize Arc multipliers and expose GBs to external snipers.  
* **1.9 Guild Threads (Arc Boosted)**: The gold standard for GB progression. Investors with a Level 80 Arc pay **1.9 times the base FP reward** for a spot (Positions 1 through 5). Upon level completion, investors recoup 100% of their investment while collecting free blueprints and medals.

### 1.9 Priming & Lock Formulas

**1.9 Priming** (or securing) is the process where a GB owner self-funds enough Forge Points into their building so that when a donor contributes at the 1.9 rate, no outside sniper can outbid them.  
**The Golden Rule of Priming (Lock Condition)**: A position \\\\(N\\\\) is secured (locked) when the total FPs on the GB meet this threshold:  
*Total FPs required on GB before Spot N is taken \= Total Level Cost (T) \- (2 × P\_N)*

#### Step-by-Step Priming Formulas

* **Priming Positions 1 & 2 (P1 & P2)**: For almost all GBs, P1 and P2 are locked together using:  
  *Owner Priming for P1 & P2 \= T \- P\_1 \- (2 × P\_2)*  
* **Priming Position 3 (P3)**: Once P1 and P2 are taken by donors, calculate:  
  *Owner Total for P3 \= T \- P\_1 \- P\_2 \- (2 × P\_3)*  
  *Additional Owner FPs to add \= Owner Total for P3 \- Owner FPs already added*  
* **Priming Subsequent Spots**: Repeat the formula for remaining spots as prior positions fill:  
  *Owner Total for Spot N \= T \- (Sum of previous P\_k) \- (2 × P\_N)*

#### The Mid-Level "Sweet Spot"

Between roughly Levels 30 and 70 (or 29–74), donor payouts cover almost the entire leveling cost. During this window, the formula \\\\(T \- P\_1 \- 2(P\_2)\\\\) yields zero or a negative number, meaning **0 owner FPs are required** to lock P1 and P2\! For instance, leveling an Alcatraz from Level 30 to 50 costs 35,621 total FPs, but using a 1.9 thread reduces the owner's out-of-pocket cost to just **4,544 FPs**.

#### Concrete Priming Calculation Example (Alcatraz Level 10 → 11\)

* **Total Level Cost (T)**: 851 FPs  
* **Base Rewards**: R1 \= 95 FPs, R2 \= 50 FPs, R3 \= 15 FPs  
* **1.9 Bids**: P1 \= 181 FPs (95 × 1.9), P2 \= 95 FPs (50 × 1.9), P3 \= 29 FPs (15 × 1.9)  
* **P1 & P2 Prime**: 851 \- 181 \- (2 × 95\) \= 480 (or **489 FPs** with exact base rounding). Owner places 489 FPs and posts P1(181) P2(95).  
* **P3 Prime**: Cumulative total needed is 851 \- 181 \- 95 \- (2 × 29\) \= **517 FPs**. Since 489 FPs were placed, the owner adds **28 more FPs** (517 \- 489\) and posts P3(29).

## **4\. Preventing Snipes & Best Practices**

### How Sniping Works

A **snipe** occurs when an outside player deposits FPs into your Great Building to lock a top reward position at a discount below the 1.9 value. Because the sniper pays less than the 1.9 rate while leveraging their Level 80 Arc (+90%), they profit off your building. When a snipe occurs, the owner loses expected donor coverage and must pay the remaining FP deficit out of pocket.

### Prime Targets for Sniping

* **Neighborhood Players**: Neighbors actively look for un-primed or poorly locked GBs.  
* **Self-Levelers & Swap Thread Users**: Adding points gradually without securing donor spots leaves GBs highly vulnerable.  
* **"Overprimers"**: Owners who deposit points for all 5 spots at once open lower spots to snipers who can lock them at cheap rates.

### Critical Best Practices

* ⚠️ **Avoid Overpriming**: Do **not** prime all 5 positions at the start of a level. Depositing points for P3, P4, or P5 before P1 and P2 are filled exposes lower spots to snipers.  
* 🔄 **Prime in Stages**: Always secure and post P1/P2 first, wait for donors to fill them, then prime and post P3, and repeat.

🛠️ **Use Online Calculators**: Use automated tools like **FOE Tools** (foe.tools) or the **FoE Helper** browser extension to instantly compute exact required owner priming deposits and generate copyable thread text.
